1. Welcome to the writing coach!

Instructor comments are guidance that can help you strengthen your academic writing. You do not need to fix everything at once. In this lesson, you will learn a simple process: Read → Explain → Revise → Check.

  • Read: Read the feedback comment and the passage it refers to.
  • Explain: Put the comment into your own words.
  • Revise: Make one focused change.
  • Check: Confirm that the change addresses the comment.

Plan: Learn the strategy, complete three short practice activities, revise a paragraph, and complete the final check.

2. Learn the strategy

Four common types of academic writing feedback

1. Add detail: Add a specific example, relevant detail, or credible source when appropriate.
2. Explain your evidence: Explain how evidence supports your claim or central idea.
3. Organize ideas: Arrange ideas logically and use transitions to show relationships.
4. Check clarity: Use precise language so readers can understand your meaning.

8. Wrap-up: Your revision toolkit

When you receive feedback, remember:

  1. Read the feedback comment and the passage it refers to.
  2. Explain the comment in your own words.
  3. Make a focused revision.
  4. Reread and check that the revision responds to the comment.

Key takeaway: Strong revision is not simply correcting errors. It means using feedback to make ideas clearer, better supported, and easier for a reader to follow.
